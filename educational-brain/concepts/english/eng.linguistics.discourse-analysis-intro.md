## Learning Objective

The learner can:
1. Identify at least three cohesive devices (pronoun reference, conjunctions, lexical repetition) in a short text and what each links back to.
2. Identify the specific point where coherence breaks down in a cohesive-but-not-fully-coherent passage.
3. Mark turn changes in a transcribed conversation and identify the specific signal that preceded each.
4. Identify where topic shifts occur in a longer conversation and what signaled each shift.
5. Fully analyze a new text or conversation, identifying cohesive devices, evaluating coherence separately, and (for conversations) identifying turn-taking and topic-management structure.

## Core Understanding

This concept extends `eng.linguistics.pragmatics-intro`'s single-utterance context-dependent meaning to how meaning and coherence are constructed across stretches of connected text or talk. Two corrections define it. First, **a text with lots of cohesive devices like pronouns and conjunctions is not automatically coherent** — COHESION (surface-level linking devices being present) and COHERENCE (a text actually making logical sense) are separate properties; a text can be dense with pronouns and conjunctions while pronoun referents are unclear or the logical connections signaled don't actually hold, and a shorter text with fewer devices can still cohere clearly. Second, **conversational turn-taking does not just happen randomly with no underlying structure** — discourse analysts have identified real, describable patterns in how speakers signal turn completion (falling intonation, a closing phrase, a grammatically complete point) that reliably prompt the next speaker to begin; these patterns are systematic and analyzable, not random social chaos.

## Mental Models

**Beginner — "a text with lots of pronouns and conjunctions is automatically coherent; conversational turn-taking just happens randomly with no structure."**
The simulation: judge a text's clarity by counting linking devices, and view who speaks when in conversation as unpredictable social chaos.
*Upgrade trigger*: the Blueprint's own conflict evidence — a passage packed with cohesive devices that fails to make sense versus a shorter, clearer passage with fewer devices, asked which is actually easier to follow; and a transcribed conversation with specific completion signals reliably prompting speaker handoffs, asked whether this looks random or systematic.
*Shelf-life warning*: "Cohesion and coherence are separate — check both. Turn-taking follows real, signaled patterns."

**Intermediate — "I can separate cohesion from coherence now, but I still tend to view conversational turn-taking as unstructured unless someone points out a specific signal to me."**
The simulation: correctly evaluates written text for cohesion versus coherence but hasn't yet reliably applied systematic pattern-detection to spoken conversation.
*Upgrade trigger*: TA-3's direct practice marking turn changes and identifying the preceding signal in a transcribed conversation.
*Shelf-life warning*: "Every turn change has a signal — intonation, a closing phrase, a completion point. Find it."

**Advanced — "I identify cohesive devices, separately evaluate coherence, identify turn-taking signals, track topic management, and fully analyze a new text or conversation integrating all of this."**
The simulation: integrates cohesion/coherence separation with conversational-structure analysis into one coherent discourse-level analysis. Matches TA-5.
*Upgrade trigger*: the two direct unlocks — corpus linguistics (analyzing large collections of real language use, drawing on discourse-level skills) and advanced discourse markers (a more specialized study of structure-signaling words and phrases).
*Shelf-life warning*: "This gives you the discourse-level analysis toolkit. Corpus linguistics scales this up to large collections; discourse markers studies the signaling words in more depth."

**Expert — "at fluency, cohesion/coherence separation and turn-taking-pattern recognition happen as an integrated part of any discourse-level reasoning."**
Named to mark the arc; the explicit identify-separate-structure routine is a scaffold for a habit that, at true fluency, is inseparable from doing discourse analysis at all.

## Why Students Fail

The dominant mechanism is that **the presence of linking words is the most immediately visible textual feature**, so counting or noticing them is a much easier, faster heuristic than actually tracing each pronoun's referent or verifying each logical connective's validity — the surface signal substitutes for the deeper check.

The second mechanism is that **turn-taking's signals (intonation, pause timing, grammatical completion) operate below the threshold of most listeners' conscious attention**, since fluent conversation feels effortless and automatic; the systematic structure is invisible until a deliberate transcript-based, slowed-down analysis is performed.

## Misconceptions

The Blueprint's **Component 1 — Misconception Register** owns trigger signals, conflict evidence, bridge/replacement text, and discrimination pairs for both entries below. Reused by reference; birth-type classification and teaching consequence added here.

### MC-A-A-TEXT-WITH-LOTS-OF-COHESIVE-DEVICES-LIKE-PRONOUNS-AND-CONJUNCTIONS-IS-AUTOMATICALLY-COHERENT
*(Blueprint Component 1, MC-A — includes the dense-cohesive-incoherent/clear-coherent discrimination pair.)*
- **Birth type**: Type 2 (perceptual intuition), from linking-device presence being the most immediately visible textual feature, mistaken for the deeper logical-sense property (coherence) that actually determines clarity.
- **Teaching consequence**: the repair requires **direct tracing of pronoun referents and logical connections in a densely cohesive but incoherent passage**, since the fix requires the learner to verify (not just count) the devices' actual function.
- **Verification of death**: given a passage that is cohesive but not fully coherent, the learner identifies the specific point where coherence breaks down despite the presence of linking devices.

### MC-B-CONVERSATIONAL-TURN-TAKING-JUST-HAPPENS-RANDOMLY-WITH-NO-UNDERLYING-STRUCTURE
*(Blueprint Component 1, second entry — includes the completion-signal discrimination pair.)*
- **Birth type**: Type 2 (perceptual intuition), from turn-taking signals operating below conscious awareness during fluent conversation, making the underlying systematic structure invisible without deliberate, slowed-down transcript analysis.
- **Teaching consequence**: the repair requires **direct marking of turn changes and their preceding signals across multiple transcript instances**, since the fix requires the systematic pattern to be empirically demonstrated, not merely asserted.
- **Verification of death**: given a transcribed conversation, the learner marks each turn change and identifies the specific signal that preceded it.

## Analogies

**Best — the Bolted-Bridge and Busy-Intersection Anchor** (Blueprint Component 3): a bridge covered in visible bolts that nonetheless fails structurally because the bolts don't connect to anything solid directly seeds the cohesion-versus-coherence fix; a busy intersection functioning through a real, systematic set of signals despite no single director directly seeds the turn-taking-structure fix.
*Breaking point*: a bridge's structural failure is an objective, physically verifiable engineering fact, while a text's "coherence breakdown" involves more interpretive judgment about whether a logical connection genuinely holds — useful for the visible-connectors-don't-guarantee-function intuition, less precise for implying coherence judgments are as unambiguous as structural failure.
*Breaking point (intersection)*: traffic signals and right-of-way rules are formally codified and externally enforced, while conversational turn-taking conventions are informal, unstated, and learned implicitly — useful for the hidden-systematic-structure intuition, less precise for the degree of formal codification involved.

**Alternative — a musical ensemble's improvisation versus random noise.** Skilled jazz musicians trading solos follow real, learned cues (a nod, a musical phrase signaling "your turn") that look spontaneous to an untrained ear but are systematic to those who know the genre's conventions — much like conversational turn-taking's invisible-but-real signals.
*Breaking point*: jazz improvisation conventions are a specialized, trained skill within a specific musical genre, while conversational turn-taking is a universal, unconsciously-acquired competency every fluent speaker already has.

**Visual anchor — the Bolted-Bridge and Busy-Intersection Anchor**, as the primary teaching metaphor and working instrument.

### ANTI-ANALOGIES (do not use)

- **"The more pronouns and transition words a text has, the clearer it is."** Directly installs MC-A as a stated rule.
- **"Who talks when in a conversation is basically random — it just happens."** Directly installs MC-B as a stated claim.

## Discovery Questions

Well suited to **guided discovery**, since both discourse-level facts are directly checkable by tracing referents/connections and by marking turn changes against their preceding signals.

1. **Need**: "If a passage has lots of pronouns and 'therefore's, does that guarantee it actually makes sense?"
2. **Playground**: the learner traces several pronoun referents in a dense passage freely before being told the official cohesion/coherence distinction.
3. **Invention**: "Can you find the specific signal — a pause, a phrase, an intonation shift — that happens right before each speaker change in this transcript?"
4. **Collision**: present a densely cohesive but incoherent passage; ask where coherence specifically breaks down.
5. **Formalization**: name the two discourse-level facts — cohesion and coherence are separate; turn-taking follows real, signaled patterns — from the learner's own testing.
6. **Compression**: "Linking words don't guarantee sense — and every turn change has a real signal."

## Voice Teaching Notes

Core evidence is **the learner's stated reasoning for a coherence judgment or a turn-taking signal identification**, since a correct-looking coherence rating with no traced referents, or a correctly-marked turn change with no cited signal, cannot be distinguished from a lucky guess.

What the ideal tutor perceives:

- **A stated referent-tracing check** ("this pronoun points back to..., and does that connection actually make sense?") applied to a new passage — the direct behavioral signature of the cohesion/coherence fix taking hold.
- **A specific signal cited before a marked turn change** ("the falling pitch there, and the sentence was grammatically complete") — direct evidence the turn-taking-structure fix is internalized.
- **A coherence judgment based purely on counting linking words, or a turn change marked with no cited signal** — the direct behavioral signature of either misconception still active.

**Load-bearing sentence, delivered slowly**: *"Linking words alone don't prove sense — and every turn change has a real, findable signal."*

## Tutor Recovery Strategy

Generic engine: `../foundations/01-recovery-engine.md`. Concept-specific notes only:

- **"This passage has lots of 'therefore's and 'howevers,' so it must make sense"** — take this as a reasonable inference from surface density; respond by checking the connections: "Let's trace each one — does the logical relationship it signals actually hold between these two ideas?"
- **"People just start talking whenever they want in a conversation"** — a reasonable-sounding impression of fluent, effortless conversation; respond by slowing it down: "Let's look at this transcript — right before each speaker change, is there a specific signal, like a pause or a completed sentence?"
- **Smaller question to shrink to**: from a full new-text-or-conversation analysis down to **tracing a single pronoun's referent in one sentence pair, with no coherence-evaluation or turn-taking task demanded.** Isolates the most basic cohesive-device-tracing skill before returning to fuller analysis.
- **Never shrink to "just count how many linking words are in this passage."** This directly re-licenses the device-density-equals-sense pattern this concept specifically works to dismantle.
