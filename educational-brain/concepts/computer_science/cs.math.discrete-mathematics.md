# Discrete Mathematics Foundations — `cs.math.discrete-mathematics`

## Identity
- Prerequisites: `cs.algo.problem-solving`, `cs.found.boolean-logic`
- Unlocks: proof techniques; sets, relations, and functions
- Difficulty / Bloom: developing / understand

## Learning objective
Interpret propositions and basic discrete structures and state assumptions precisely.

## Core understanding
Computer science reasons about finite objects, collections, relations, and sequences. A proposition has a truth value; a predicate becomes a proposition when its variables are specified or quantified. Boolean logic is one part of discrete mathematics, not the entire subject.

## Misconceptions
- **M1 — Discrete mathematics is only Boolean logic.** Correction: it also includes sets, relations, functions, counting, and proof methods.
- **M2 — A claim that works in many examples is universally proven.** Correction: examples do not rule out all counterexamples; a universal claim needs proof.

## Teaching and assessment notes
Start with small finite examples, require the learner to state definitions, and vary one condition to test transfer. Assess assumptions and reasoning rather than symbol recognition alone.
