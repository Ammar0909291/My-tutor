## Rules and traps that have cost time (condensed)
- **Reserved, must stay figure-less:** `math.linalg.vector-addition` (AI canary) and `math.func.linear-function` (fixture other tests use). Any test that needs a figure-less concept uses one of those two.
- **SM-01** (figure audit) evaluates the arithmetic in a label **left to right** and flags labels that share a last token before "=" with different numbers: never write `1 × 5 + 2 × 7 = 19` as one label;
  `≈` counts as `=`; `π(100)` is read as π × 100 — use words ("25 primes up to 100"). One label per product / sum.
- **Budget:** at most 9 *named* labels per figure (labels of 1-2 characters, numerals included, are free); at least one named label; <= 50 objects per step; exactly 3 cumulative steps; all coordinates within ±5;
  role colours only (`ROLE.*`); `curve()` needs >= 8 points; no zero-length lines/arrows. `P()` rounds to 0.01 — read-back tolerances must scale with a steep curve's slope.
- **Label solver:** a label is shoved away from any line, dot or other label near it. Tables and rulers need >= 0.75 × 0.65 world units per cell, no line/outline/cross-out within ~0.35 of an entry — mark with colour, or a dot offset >= 0.4 /
  radius >= 0.13. Never cross out a letter; put the cross between rows (rows >= 1.2 apart). Check with `label-displacement.ts`. Nothing may be *unresolved* on card or phone hosts (measured 0 for all 516 figures).
- **Caption placement:** the solver moves a caption that crosses the 570 px card edge, shares a row with a neighbour within a few px, or sits <0.9 world units from another caption: centre a ~30-character caption (≈290 px) where both ends stay inside the card and check `label-displacement.ts` (0 card-host moves) before rendering. A truncated curve has a smaller sd than the true one.
- **Drawn-value read-back:** `P()` rounds to 0.01, so small values (y < 0.4) carry large relative error: fit/ratio/ln checks only on the tall part of a curve, and a central difference of drawn points carries ±0.07 noise. A bar or line shorter than 0.005 rounds to zero length (ST-07): do not draw it. SM-01 reads `W(0) = 1` as W × 0: avoid `f(x) =` captions that clash with another `f =` caption.
- **Tests:** `toBe` fails on `-0` vs `0` and on floats (use `toBeCloseTo`); pair box edges by their known height (`rectBoxes(..., height)`); normalise U+2212 "−" before parsing negatives; skip exact-zero samples when counting sign changes
  (`crossings`); an `as Pt` on an array-of-arrays lookup needs `as const` (the type-error ratchet will catch it).
- **Never** run a production write, log a credential, or push to any branch other than `main`. The harness-designated branch `claude/physics-verifier-batch-4-9tgycs` is 1,489 commits behind main — moving it needs a force push and an explicit owner decision (not done).
