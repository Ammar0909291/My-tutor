# Chemistry learner-experience run on the deployed app (2026-10-14)

Owner request: take 5 Chemistry concepts as a learner on a given real account, rate them and share the experience, observing the visuals. Driver: `scripts/qa/chemistryLearnerExperience.ts` (credentials only from `QA_EMAIL` / `QA_PASSWORD` in the environment of one invocation; never written or logged; nothing deleted). It plays each lesson turn by turn (quick checks answered from the authored probe corpus — right, except the 2nd card, answered wrongly on purpose — plus "show me a diagram" / "explain differently" / "practice question"), records the wire payloads, then opens the real `/learn` page in Chromium (390 px dark + light, 1280 px) and screenshots every figure frame. Account state before: 87 of 186 Chemistry lessons untouched (96 COMPLETED). The 5 lessons chosen had no progress row (electronic-config was IN_PROGRESS from an aborted first attempt).

Result: 5/5 lessons reached verified mastery in 14 turns each (mean reply 15.4 s, max 22.9 s). All five lessons ended by re-asking the FIRST quick check verbatim (the owner-approved one-re-ask rule: a correct answer given in GUIDE banks no credit, `recordMcqOutcome`).

## Fixes made after the run (2026-10-14, owner: "fix the defects")
| Defect | Root cause (evidence) | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| The lesson's figure re-attached on turn 8 (4 of 5 lessons; it is the same pattern as F2 in `chemistry-modern-periodic-law-qa-2026-10-14.md`) | `decideContinuity`'s safety valve (`MAX_EXCURSION_TURNS` = 6, written as a ceiling on a DETOUR) also fired for the lesson's OWN figure: introduced turn 1, held turns 2-7, released on turn 8, then re-resolved as a NEW figure (`session.turns === 0`) and attached to that reply. Reproduced on production with a fresh lesson (`chem.period.atomic-radius`: figure on turns 1, 2, 8, 9) and by mutation (the new test fails with "introduced on turns 1,8 … excursion-turn-limit" when the fix is removed) | The valve applies only when the held figure is not the lesson's concept (`session.ts`) | `visualContinuity.test.ts` (2 new) |
| Graph: the x-axis title ("Volume (L)") was pushed into the zoom buttons at desktop width | The legibility pass's label solver only sees the SVG; it moved the title 12 px down (`transform: translate(0,12px)`, measured) into the HTML buttons (title bottom 331 px, buttons top 326 px) | An invisible rect over the button zone is declared as an obstacle (`GraphRenderer.tsx`, `data-controls-zone`) | `visual2dLayoutSafety.test.ts` (2 new); measured after: title bottom 307 px vs buttons top 326 px (1280), 237 vs 244 (390) |
| Graph: P = 10/V on 1-10 opened with its high-pressure end cut off and half the plot below zero (tick "-2 atm") | `initialGraphView` always centres y on 0 at the x-domain scale | For a curve that stays strictly one side of zero AND is clipped by that view, the view is fitted from zero using the frame's real height (re-fit until the learner pans/zooms). Curves that fit, or cross zero (y = x, x², sin), keep their view EXACTLY. The server's admission check still measures the original view, so a graph empty there is still refused | `generatedGraphMustShowItsCurve.test.ts` (3 new, incl. a property test: the fitted view never shows less of a curve than the original) |
Audit impact: no served Tier 0/1 figure in any subject uses the graph renderer; of the Chemistry Tier 2/3 snapshot's graph specs only 6 would open differently and 5 of those are NO_FIGURE in the committed audit (the 6th is a Tier 0 generator figure), so no committed audit record is made stale.

Not fixed, with the reason:
- Cl radius "99 pm" in the tutor's words vs 79 pm on the figure: model wording about a number; checking it is the numeric verification the owner closed (2026-10-04) — needs a fresh owner instruction.
- Cards whose options carry their own working/error label (enthalpy), the long-correct-option 2-option card (electronic configuration): authored content. Only 7 of 931 authored chemistry options use the `head: working` shape and one such set is a plain ratio ("2 : 1"), so extending `splitAnswerHeads` would risk false positives for almost no gain; the real fix is editing the authored probes, which the create-only bootstrap will not push to production without an owner-approved content sync.
- Prose practice problem vs a different attached card (enthalpy turn 11): model behaviour against the gate's "lead-in only" instruction; a deterministic trim would risk cutting real teaching.
- VSEPR water: the two lone pairs are physically correct (above and below the bond plane) and so project to one point from the default camera; fixing it means a different default camera for every molecule figure and a full re-audit.
- Graph on a phone: the steep start of a curve can touch the y-axis title (it keeps its halo); a design decision for the graph frame.
